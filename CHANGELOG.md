# Changelog

Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`; the build number in `pubspec.yaml` goes up by one with every release.

## 1.0.0 — unreleased

First release of the rebuilt app (Android APK; iOS builds but is not distributed).

- Accounts: email and Google sign-in, password reset, profile with role and birth year (13+ for children), language switch (中文 / English), account deletion inside the app.
- Families: create a family, invite code (7 days), join requests approved by a parent, leave, remove a member, the family is deleted with its last parent.
- Assessment: 24 questions per role, Warmth and Demandingness scores, parenting style, resumable drafts. A child's answers stay private; parents see only scores and style.
- Compare and History: the parent's self view and each child's view on one chart, perception gap per dimension, history lines.
- Advice library: 25 cards per role and style, filter by style and topic.
- Challenges: start a one-week family challenge from any advice card, daily check-ins, totals at the end of the week, live activity list.
- Reminders: weekly check-in and daily challenge check-in notifications, restored after a reboot.
- Privacy policy in Chinese and English, crash reports from release builds (no personal content).
