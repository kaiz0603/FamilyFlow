# FamilyFlow Privacy Policy

[中文](privacy-policy-zh.html) · Last updated: 26 November 2026

FamilyFlow is a free app for parents and for children aged 13 and over. It has no ads, and we do not sell or share your data.

## What we store
- Your email address and how you sign in (email and password, or Google).
- Your display name, role (parent or child), birth year and language.
- The family you belong to.
- Your questionnaire answers. Only you can see them.
- Your scores and parenting style. Members of your family can see them.
- Family challenges, daily check-ins and the family activity list.
- Crash reports from release builds: the error, device model, Android version and app version. They contain no names, emails or answers.

On your phone only: your reminder settings and an unfinished questionnaire.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, phone number, full birth date or advertising ID, and we use no analytics or advertising services.

## Who can see it
Members of your family see your display name, role, scores, style, challenges and check-ins. Nobody else can see your individual answers, including your parents or children in the app. We, as the operator, can technically access the stored data and look at it only to keep the app running or when the law requires it.

## Where it is stored
In Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Crashlytics), operated by Google LLC as our data processor. Firestore data is stored in Singapore (asia-southeast1).

## How long we keep it
- Family data: until you leave the family. When the last parent leaves, the whole family is deleted.
- Everything else: until you delete your account.
- Activity entries: deleted automatically after 90 days.
- Crash reports: 90 days.

## Your choices
You can delete your account and all your data inside the app at any time: Settings → Delete account. You can leave a family at any time: Settings → Leave family.

## Children
FamilyFlow is not for children under 13. Sign-up asks for the birth year and stops if the user is under 13. Because only the year is asked, someone a few months short of 13 could get through. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we update the date above and the policy here. Important changes are also announced in the release notes.
