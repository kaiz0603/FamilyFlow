# FamilyFlow

A free app for families with teenagers. A parent rates their own parenting, each child (13 and over) rates each parent, and the family sees the perception gap on one chart. Then you pick a one-week challenge together and check in every day. No ads, and your data is never sold or shared.

**[Download for Android](https://github.com/kaiz0603/FamilyFlow/releases/latest)**: get `FamilyFlow-vX.Y.Z.apk` (most phones) or the `-armv7` file for older phones, then allow your browser to install apps when Android asks. iOS is not available.

# FamilyFlow（中文）

一款给有青少年的家庭用的免费 App。父母给自己的教养方式打分，每个孩子（13 岁以上）给每位父母打分，全家在同一张图上看到彼此看法的差距。然后一起选一个为期一周的挑战，每天打卡。没有广告，你的数据不会被出售或分享。

**[下载 Android 版](https://github.com/kaiz0603/FamilyFlow/releases/latest)**：下载 `FamilyFlow-vX.Y.Z.apk`（大多数手机），较旧的手机下载 `-armv7` 文件；安装时如果 Android 询问，允许浏览器安装应用。暂无 iOS 版。
